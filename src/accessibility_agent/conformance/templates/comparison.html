How to read change statuses

New
A current finding classified as new by the comparison analysis.
Still present
The same finding identity is observed in both scans.
Resolved
The comparison analysis found adequate recorded verification evidence for the absent baseline finding. Check its reason and scope.
Not verified
The baseline finding was not re-observed, but available coverage or rule results do not establish resolution.
Newly observed
The current finding was newly observed without sufficient comparable baseline coverage to establish a regression.
Offline evidence comparison · No conformance or certification decision is inferred from these counts.
